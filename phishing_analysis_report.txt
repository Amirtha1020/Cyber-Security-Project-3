DECODELABS – CYBER SECURITY PROJECT 3
PHISHING AWARENESS ANALYSIS

Objective:
Analyze a sample message and identify indicators of a phishing attempt.

SAMPLE PHISHING MESSAGE

Subject: Urgent: Your Account Will Be Suspended

From: security-alert@example-support.com

Message:
Dear Customer,

We detected unusual activity on your account. Your account will be suspended within 24 hours unless you verify your information immediately.

Click here to verify your account:
http://example-security-verify.com/login

Please enter your username, password, and other account details to complete verification.

Thank you,
Security Team


ANALYSIS

1. Suspicious sender
The sender address uses a generic-looking domain instead of a clearly verified official organization domain.

2. Urgent language
The message says the account will be suspended within 24 hours. This creates fear and pressure so the recipient may act without checking.

3. Suspicious link
The verification link points to a domain that does not clearly belong to the claimed service.

4. Request for sensitive information
The message asks for a username, password, and other account details. Legitimate services generally should not request passwords through an unsolicited email link.

5. Threat of account suspension
The warning about immediate suspension is a common social-engineering technique used to make the victim panic.

6. Unexpected verification request
The recipient is asked to verify the account through an unsolicited message rather than using the organization's known official website or app.

RED FLAGS FOUND

• Untrusted or suspicious sender domain
• Urgent 24-hour deadline
• Threat of account suspension
• Suspicious external URL
• Request for username and password
• Unexpected account verification request
• Fear and pressure used to encourage immediate action

WHY THE MESSAGE IS UNSAFE

The message contains several phishing indicators. It attempts to create urgency and fear, directs the recipient to a suspicious website, and requests sensitive login information. Following the link or entering credentials could expose the account information to an attacker.

SAFE ACTIONS

• Do not click the suspicious link.
• Do not provide passwords or other sensitive information.
• Verify the message using the organization's official website or app.
• Report the message as phishing/spam.
• Delete the message after reporting it.

CONCLUSION

Phishing messages often use urgency, suspicious links, impersonation, threats, and requests for sensitive information. Checking the sender, URL, wording, and requested information before taking action can help prevent phishing attacks.
